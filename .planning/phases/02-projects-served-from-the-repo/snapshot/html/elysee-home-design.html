Élysée Home Design

View live project↗Start a project→

Brand and Framer site for a new Dubai interior design studio. A project showcase they can update from the CMS.

01

Overview

Élysée Home Design is a Dubai interior studio for homes, offices, and commercial spaces: concept, 3D, materials, and site supervision. The client is Wafa Dridi, the founder. Branded by Khadija. I designed and built the site in Framer: projects, process, team, contact, and a CMS so they can add work themselves. It is a showcase, not a booking product.

02

Challenge

A new business. Nothing existed yet. They needed a brand and a site that could show interiors clearly and stay current without a rebuild each time a project finished. February 2026.

03

Outcome

The site is live at elyseehomedesign.com. Brand, pages, and CMS are in their hands. Inquiry is on the site.
